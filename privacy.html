Privacy Policy

Elvis Drive Backup — Updated: 5 October 2026

Purpose

Elvis Drive Backup is a personal setup operated by the account owner. It uses rclone to synchronize a designated external-drive folder with a configured Google Drive destination.

Google account access

The owner authorizes access through Google's sign-in process. The requested Google Drive permission allows broad access to Drive files. The synchronization job is configured to operate on a designated destination folder.

Rclone processes file contents and metadata, such as names, sizes and modification times, to compare and transfer files. Mirror synchronization may update or delete destination files to match the source.

Storage and use

Files are transferred directly between the owner's computers and Google Drive. Backup files remain on the external drive and in Google Drive according to the owner's configuration.

OAuth authorization tokens are stored in the local rclone configuration on configured computers. Local synchronization logs may contain file names, paths and operation results.

Google user data is used only for this backup and synchronization purpose. The operator does not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

This website

This informational website does not receive backup files, Google passwords or OAuth tokens. It contains no custom analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process visitor technical information under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Control and removal

The owner can stop scheduled synchronization and revoke the application's access through their Google Account security settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing backup files.

The owner controls deletion of cloud copies, local configuration, authorization tokens and logs. Google Drive retention and trash behavior are governed by Google and the account's settings.

Contact

Contact the operator through elvisleeky-dev on GitHub. Do not post credentials or private file information publicly.
